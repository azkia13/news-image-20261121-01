A one-image front page summing up Saturday, 21 November 2026 — drawn
deterministically with Python and PIL, no network, no randomness, no
timestamps. Two runs produce byte-identical PNG output.

## The day in brief

**Lead:** NATO vows to defend every inch of Poland after Russian missiles
cross its border — Secretary General Mark Rutte in Warsaw, a day after
Poland said three Russian objects (probably two missiles and a drone)
entered its airspace during a huge overnight attack on Ukraine.

**Also on the page:** Russian missiles kill a 7-year-old boy and a
paramedic in Kryvyi Rih; the S&P 500 falls 3.7% into correction as the AI
sell-off spreads; Mexico captures FBI most-wanted cartel boss "El Chapo
Isidro"; COP31 climate talks run past their deadline with fossil fuels
missing from the draft deal; US measles cases near 6,000; Trump vows a
10% cap on credit-card interest rates; and Wolbachia mosquitoes cut
dengue infections by about 69% in a Brazilian trial of 5,700 children.

## Sources

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py        # writes /output/entry.png (1024x1024, <= 1 MiB)
```

Requires Pillow (tested with PIL 10.2.0). The output is deterministic:
run it twice and the SHA-256 checksums match.
